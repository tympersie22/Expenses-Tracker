Expenses Tracker gives you one clear place to understand the money you record.

See what is available after upcoming bills and savings goals. Record income, expenses, and transfers in their original currencies. Import a statement for review, scan a receipt on your device, find recurring activity, and explore the next 30 days before you spend.

Your records stay under your control. Sessions are revocable, two-factor authentication is available, and you can download a complete archive or permanently delete your account from Settings.

Expenses Tracker does not move money or claim to show a live bank balance. Bank, card, and mobile-money connections are not enabled in this release.
